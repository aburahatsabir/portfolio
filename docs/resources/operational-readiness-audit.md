# 10-Point Operational Readiness Audit

## Executive Operations Health Check

**Free Download** — Assess your organization's operational maturity in 10 minutes.

---

## Instructions

Rate each statement from 1-5:
- **1** = Strongly Disagree / Major Issue
- **2** = Disagree / Significant Gap
- **3** = Neutral / Some Progress
- **4** = Agree / Mostly Resolved
- **5** = Strongly Agree / Fully Optimized

---

### 1. Process Automation
**"Our repetitive administrative tasks are automated, not manual."**

- [ ] 1 — Most tasks are manual
- [ ] 2 — Some automation exists
- [ ] 3 — 50% automated
- [ ] 4 — 75% automated
- [ ] 5 — 90%+ automated

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 2 indicates 10+ hours/week of recoverable time

---

### 2. Month-End Close Efficiency
**"Our month-end financial close takes less than 2 business days."**

- [ ] 1 — Takes 5+ days
- [ ] 2 — Takes 3-4 days
- [ ] 3 — Takes 2-3 days
- [ ] 4 — Takes 1-2 days
- [ ] 5 — Takes < 1 day

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 3 indicates process bottlenecks costing $5K+/month

---

### 3. Data Integrity
**"We have zero manual data entry errors in critical systems."**

- [ ] 1 — Frequent errors
- [ ] 2 — Occasional errors
- [ ] 3 — Rare errors
- [ ] 4 — Very rare errors
- [ ] 5 — Zero errors (validated)

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 2 indicates audit risk and potential compliance issues

---

### 4. Systems Integration
**"Our systems communicate automatically without manual data transfer."**

- [ ] 1 — All manual transfers
- [ ] 2 — Mostly manual
- [ ] 3 — 50% automated
- [ ] 4 — 75% automated
- [ ] 5 — Fully integrated

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 2 indicates 15+ hours/week of redundant work

---

### 5. Audit Readiness
**"We can produce audit-ready reports in under 30 minutes."**

- [ ] 1 — Takes days
- [ ] 2 — Takes hours
- [ ] 3 — Takes 1-2 hours
- [ ] 4 — Takes 30-60 min
- [ ] 5 — Takes < 30 min

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 3 indicates governance gaps and compliance risk

---

### 6. Executive Time Optimization
**"Our executives spend < 20% of time on administrative tasks."**

- [ ] 1 — 60%+ on admin
- [ ] 2 — 40-60% on admin
- [ ] 3 — 20-40% on admin
- [ ] 4 — 10-20% on admin
- [ ] 5 — < 10% on admin

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 3 indicates $50K+/year in misallocated executive time

---

### 7. Vendor Independence
**"We own our core operational logic and aren't locked into SaaS vendors."**

- [ ] 1 — Fully vendor-dependent
- [ ] 2 — Mostly vendor-dependent
- [ ] 3 — 50% owned logic
- [ ] 4 — 75% owned logic
- [ ] 5 — Full sovereignty

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 2 indicates $20K+/year in unnecessary SaaS costs

---

### 8. Scalability Without Headcount
**"We can handle 2x workload without hiring additional admin staff."**

- [ ] 1 — Would need 3+ hires
- [ ] 2 — Would need 2 hires
- [ ] 3 — Would need 1 hire
- [ ] 4 — Could manage with contractors
- [ ] 5 — Current systems scale

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 3 indicates $100K+/year in future hiring costs

---

### 9. Institutional Knowledge Capture
**"Our processes are documented and not dependent on specific people."**

- [ ] 1 — Entirely tribal knowledge
- [ ] 2 — Mostly undocumented
- [ ] 3 — 50% documented
- [ ] 4 — 75% documented
- [ ] 5 — Fully documented + automated

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 2 indicates critical single-point-of-failure risk

---

### 10. Real-Time Operational Visibility
**"Leadership has real-time dashboards for key operational metrics."**

- [ ] 1 — No dashboards
- [ ] 2 — Manual reports only
- [ ] 3 — Weekly dashboards
- [ ] 4 — Daily dashboards
- [ ] 5 — Real-time dashboards

**Red Flag:** Score ≤ 3 indicates reactive (not proactive) decision-making

## Scoring Guide

**Total Score: _____ / 50**

### 40-50: Operational Excellence
✅ **Status:** World-class operations  
🎯 **Action:** Maintain and optimize edge cases

### 30-39: Strong Foundation
✅ **Status:** Above-average maturity  
🎯 **Action:** Focus on automation and integration gaps

### 20-29: Significant Gaps
⚠️ **Status:** Operational inefficiencies costing $50K+/year  
🎯 **Action:** Prioritize automation and process redesign

### 10-19: Critical Risk
🚨 **Status:** Major operational debt and compliance risk  
🎯 **Action:** Immediate intervention required
